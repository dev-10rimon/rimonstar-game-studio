About RIMONSTAR
Game Studio

Independent · Original · Craft-driven

RIMONSTAR Game Studio is an independent game studio focused on creating original games and interactive experiences.

We believe games can be quiet, deliberate, and meaningful — not just loud. Our work explores the space between mechanics and emotion, where a simple interaction can become something memorable.

Every project we take on is built from the ground up with intention. We are not chasing trends. We are building experiences we would want to play ourselves.

The studio is small and independent by choice. This allows us to stay close to the work, make decisions quickly, and maintain a direct relationship with the people who play our games.

What We Value

Three Principles

Original Ideas

We start with a concept worth exploring — not a formula worth repeating. Every project asks a different question.

Gameplay

The feel of a game is everything. We iterate until the interaction itself is satisfying, not just functional.

Craft

Every detail matters — from code architecture to the curve of a single animation. We sweat the small things.

Worth Playing.
