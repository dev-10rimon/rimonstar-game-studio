Privacy Policy

Game: Mystic Spiral: Marble Quest
Effective Date: August 30, 2026
Last Updated: October 22, 2026

RIMONSTAR ("we", "us", or "our") developed Mystic Spiral: Marble Quest ("the Game") as an offline mobile game.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the Game handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Mystic Spiral: Marble Quest does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information.

The Game does not require users to provide:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Location information
  • Photos or videos
  • Contacts
  • Account credentials
  • Payment information
  • Any other personally identifiable information

The Game does not require users to create or sign in to an account.

2. Local Game Data

The Game may save game progress and settings locally on the user's device using Unity PlayerPrefs. This may include information such as:

  • Game progress
  • Level or gameplay state
  • Game settings and preferences

This information is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to RIMONSTAR or any external server. We do not have access to this locally stored game data.

If the Game is uninstalled or its local application data is cleared, locally stored game progress may be deleted.

3. Internet and Network Access

Mystic Spiral: Marble Quest is designed to function as an offline game. The Game does not require users to create an online account or connect to an online service to play. We do not operate a server that collects or stores users' personal information.

4. Advertising

Mystic Spiral: Marble Quest does not display advertisements. The Game does not use advertising SDKs or services to collect information for advertising purposes.

5. In-App Purchases

Mystic Spiral: Marble Quest does not contain in-app purchases (IAP). The Game does not collect or process payment information.

6. Third-Party Services

The Game does not intentionally use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or user-data collection services. The Game is developed using the Unity game engine. Unity PlayerPrefs is used only for storing certain game progress and settings locally on the user's device.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Game is designed to be accessible and enjoyable for players of different ages. The gameplay is easy to understand while providing a moderate level of challenge that can be enjoyed by both children and older players.

The Game does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children or other users. Because the Game does not require an account and does not directly collect personal information, players can enjoy the Game without providing personal information.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child under the age of 13 has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us at support@rimonstar.com. We will take appropriate steps to address the concern and delete such information where required.

8. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit personal information, we do not maintain a database of users' personal information. Game progress stored through Unity PlayerPrefs remains on the user's device and is not sent to our servers.

9. Data Deletion

We do not maintain personal user data on our servers. Users can remove locally stored Game data by uninstalling the Game or clearing the Game's application data through their device settings, where supported.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the Game's features or data practices change. Any updated Privacy Policy will be posted at the same location with a revised "Last Updated" date.
