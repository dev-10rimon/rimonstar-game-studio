Privacy Policy for Capsule Jump: 3D Platformer

Thank you for playing Capsule Jump: 3D Platformer, developed by RIMONSTAR, an independent game developer.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile game. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the information used by the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Capsule Jump: 3D Platformer does not directly collect, store, or transmit personal information from players.

  • The Game does not require users to create an account, provide their name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to play.
  • The Game does not use online servers or require an internet connection for its core gameplay.

2. Local Game Data

The Game may store certain gameplay and preference information locally on your device using Unity's local storage system, such as PlayerPrefs.

This information may include:

  • Game progress
  • Unlocked levels
  • Level stars or completion information
  • Game settings and preferences

This information is stored locally on your device and is not directly transmitted to us. If you uninstall the Game or clear its app data, locally stored game progress and preferences may be deleted.

3. Children's Privacy

Our Game is designed to be accessible and enjoyable for players of different ages. The gameplay is easy to understand while providing a moderate level of challenge that can be enjoyed by both children and older players.

Although children may play the Game, Capsule Jump: 3D Platformer is not specifically directed toward children.

The Game does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children or other users. Because the Game does not require an account and does not directly collect personal information, players can enjoy the Game without providing personal information.

4. Advertising

The current version of Capsule Jump: 3D Platformer does not display advertisements. The Game does not use advertising services or collect information for personalized advertising in the current version.

5. In-App Purchases

The current version of Capsule Jump: 3D Platformer does not offer in-app purchases. No payment information is collected or processed by the Game.

6. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The current version of the Game does not use third-party analytics, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or similar services to collect information about players or their devices.

7. Third-Party Services

The current version of the Game does not intentionally use third-party services to collect personal information from players. If third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain how those services handle information.

8. Internet and Online Services

The core gameplay of Capsule Jump: 3D Platformer does not require an online account or connection to our servers. The Game does not provide chat, messaging, social networking, or other player-to-player communication features.

9. Data Security

Because the Game does not directly collect or transmit personal information, we do not maintain a database of players' personal information. Gameplay and preference data stored locally on the device remain under the control of the device and its operating system.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the Game's features, services, or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected on this page by updating the Last Updated date. We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
