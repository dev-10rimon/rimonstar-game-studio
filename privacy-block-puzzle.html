Privacy Policy for Block Puzzle: Green Valley

RIMONSTAR ("we", "us", or "our") developed Block Puzzle: Green Valley as a free-to-play mobile game.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how information is used, and how your information is protected.

1. Information We Collect

Block Puzzle: Green Valley does not directly collect, store, or require personal information from players.

The game does not require:

  • Account registration
  • Login or sign-in
  • Name or email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media
  • Precise location information
  • Passwords
  • Payment information

The game can be played without creating an account or providing personal information.

2. Offline Gameplay

Block Puzzle: Green Valley is designed to be playable offline. Core gameplay does not require an internet connection or user account.

Game progress and certain gameplay settings may be stored locally on your device using local storage technologies such as Unity PlayerPrefs. This information remains on your device and is not directly transmitted to us.

3. Third-Party Services

At this time, Block Puzzle: Green Valley does not use third-party advertising, analytics, social login, or online account services to collect personal information from players. If third-party services are added in a future version of the game, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Children's Privacy

Block Puzzle: Green Valley is a casual puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the game does not require account registration or the submission of personal information, players can enjoy the game without providing personal data. Parents or guardians who have questions about privacy are welcome to contact us using the contact information provided below.

5. Data Security

We do not collect or maintain a personal database of player information. Any gameplay-related data stored locally on your device is managed by the game and remains on your device unless you remove the game or clear its data.

6. Data Deletion

Because we do not collect or maintain personal information on our servers, there is no personal account or server-side personal data that users need to request us to delete. You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game from your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the game, technology, or applicable requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
